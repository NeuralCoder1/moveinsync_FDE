## Why Assumptions Matter

Writing hypotheses before looking at data locks in what counts as evidence, so I can't quietly redefine "success" after seeing the numbers. It guards against confirmation bias, where I notice the reviews that fit my story and ignore the ones that don't. If a hypothesis turns out wrong, I'll know it was a real test and not a conclusion in disguise.
